Deleting your data

You can remove what Atrovia holds from your Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp accounts at any time, and from any other account you connected. Here is what we keep and how to delete it.

What we keep from Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)

  • The access tokens you granted when you connected, stored encrypted.
  • The Pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts you chose to connect, and their posts, insights, campaigns and ad results.
  • Comments on your own posts, and the Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp conversations customers start with your business, so your team can answer them in one place.

We only read the accounts you selected, and we only post a reply when a person on your team presses Send.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, right now

In Atrovia, open Marketing → Integrations → Meta Ads → Manage and choose Disconnect and wipe all synced data. That revokes our access and deletes the synced Facebook and Instagram data for your company. For WhatsApp, revoke the token you gave us in Meta Business → Business settings → System users, and use option 3 to have the messages we stored deleted.

Option 2 — remove Atrovia from Facebook

On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select Atrovia and remove it. That stops all access straight away. To also have the data we already synced deleted, use option 1 or option 3.

Option 3 — ask us

Email admin@atrovia.co with the subject Data deletion request, the name of your business, and the Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number concerned. We will delete that data and confirm by email when it is done.

The same applies to anything else you connected (Google, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Shopify). More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
