The three things that matter most

1 · Everything the agents produce is a recommendation. Not an instruction, not a decision, and not a guarantee. Nothing an agent says commits you to anything, and nothing an agent says relieves you of deciding for yourself.

2 · The agents make mistakes. AI systems misread context, miscalculate, use a figure that is out of date, and state a wrong answer with complete confidence. This is a known characteristic of the technology, not an occasional defect. Assume any given Output may be wrong until you have checked it.

3 · You make the decision, and you own it. Every campaign you launch, every price you set, every dollar you spend, every person you hire, promote, review or let go is your decision. Review Output before you act on it. You are responsible for the outcome of decisions you make, whether or not Atrovia recommended them.
