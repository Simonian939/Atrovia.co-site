QuickBooks has been disconnected

Your QuickBooks company is no longer connected to Atrovia. Nothing in your books was changed, and nothing was deleted.

What just happened

  • Access tokens revoked. Atrovia can no longer reach your QuickBooks company.
  • All scheduled syncing stopped. No further data will be retrieved.
  • Your QuickBooks data is untouched. We only ever read it — we never posted, edited or removed anything.

What happens to the numbers in Atrovia

The reports and history we already built stay in your account — your margins, costs and past periods are still there. They simply stop updating, and any figure that was measured from your books is now as of the date you disconnected rather than current.

Some things will change while you're disconnected:

  • Cash position and profit figures will go stale and be labelled as such.
  • Costs and margins fall back to your own estimates where we have them.
  • Fin's hiring readiness signal is paused — it needs measured numbers to mean anything.

We'd rather show you a figure marked as of 12 September than a confident-looking number that quietly stopped being true.

Want your data deleted as well?

Disconnecting stops the flow. It doesn't erase what we already have — some businesses disconnect temporarily and want their history waiting when they come back.

If you'd like the financial data we retrieved deleted, email admin@atrovia.co and we'll remove it and confirm when it's done. Details in our Privacy Policy.

Changed your mind?

Reconnecting takes about a minute, and the history we already built stays intact.

If you didn't mean to disconnect

A disconnection can also happen when a QuickBooks admin removes the app, or when an authorisation expires. If this is a surprise, check with whoever administers your QuickBooks company — and if it still doesn't add up, email admin@atrovia.co and we'll look at what happened.
