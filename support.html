Getting help

One inbox, answered by the people who build Atrovia. Email admin@atrovia.co and you'll hear back the same business day.

admin@atrovia.co

No ticket number, no form to fill in, no queue to pick from. Write what happened and we'll take it from there.

Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm Pacific. Anything sent outside those hours is answered the next business morning.

What to tell us

You don't have to write a bug report. But these four things turn most emails into one reply instead of four:

The email address you sign in with
It tells us which company and which account to look at.
What you were doing
"Sending a quote to a client", "connecting QuickBooks", "adding a user".
What you expected, and what happened instead
If there was a message on screen, the exact words help — even if they look like nonsense.
Roughly when
"About 20 minutes ago" is enough to find it in our logs.

Things worth trying first

  • Something won't send. If the message mentions agreements, open Atrovia and you'll be asked to accept them once — sending works normally afterwards.
  • A connection looks broken. Settings → Connectors shows the real state of each one, and reconnecting takes about a minute.
  • A teammate never got their invitation. An owner can set their password directly from Settings → Users.
  • Figures look stale. Anything measured from a connected account is labelled with the date it was measured. If that date is old, the connection is the place to look.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the data we hold for your company at any time, whether or not you're closing the account. Email admin@atrovia.co from the address you sign in with and we'll remove it and confirm when it's done — within 30 days, and usually far sooner. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a security problem

If you've found something that looks like a security issue, email admin@atrovia.co with SECURITY in the subject line. We'd rather hear about it from you than from anyone else, and we won't argue with someone who took the trouble to tell us.
