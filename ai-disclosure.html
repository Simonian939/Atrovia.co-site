How our AI agents work

Last updated 13 September 2026 · Plain-English companion to section 8 of the End User License Agreement

Atrovia gives you a team of AI agents — for marketing, sales, operations and finance. They read your numbers, draft your plans, and tell you what they think you should do next. They are very useful and they are sometimes wrong. This page explains both, so you know exactly what you are working with.

One

It's a recommendation

Never an instruction and never a decision. Nothing an agent says commits you to anything.

Two

They make mistakes

Confidently. A wrong number can look exactly like a right one. Check before you act.

Three

You make the call

Every campaign, price, dollar and hire is your decision, and the result is yours.

What the agents actually do

Each agent reads the data in your account — what you told us during setup, what your team enters, and what we pull from the services you connect, like QuickBooks or Google. From that it produces things like:

AreaWhat the agents produce
MarketingCampaign plans, budgets, ad and email copy, content calendars, website and review recommendations
SalesPipeline analysis, follow-up suggestions, quote drafts, close-rate and forecast figures
OperationsJob stages and targets, capacity and bottleneck analysis, procedures, training assignments, scheduling suggestions
FinanceMargin and cost build-ups, pricing suggestions, revenue per person, cash and profit reporting, a readiness signal for hiring
The planYour business plan, quarterly priorities, scoreboard, and the weekly report that pulls it together

All of it is a draft for you to review. Think of an agent as a well-prepared new employee who has read everything about your business, works all night, never gets tired — and occasionally gets something badly wrong without realising it.

Where they get things wrong

We would rather tell you this up front than have you discover it:

  • Confidently wrong numbers. AI can state an incorrect figure in a perfectly reasonable sentence. There is no wobble in its voice when it's wrong.
  • Estimates that look like facts. Before you connect your books, most of your numbers are your own estimates or industry averages. We label them — but a figure that says estimate is still an estimate three screens later.
  • Out-of-date context. Data syncs on a schedule. Something that changed this morning may not be reflected yet.
  • How your books are set up. If expenses are categorised unusually in QuickBooks, a margin figure derived from them will be off — and the agent won't know that.
  • Missing context only you have. The agent doesn't know a customer is about to cancel, that your best installer is leaving, or that last month was unusual.
  • Copy that needs a human. Drafted marketing claims are drafts. You are responsible for every claim you publish about your own business.

What to always review before acting

Read these before you approve. It takes a minute and it is the whole job:

  • Ad spend — before launching a campaign or raising a budget
  • Anything public — content, social posts, review replies, website copy
  • Anything sent to a customer — emails, texts, quotes
  • Prices, discounts and margins — check the cost inputs, not just the answer
  • Financial figures — before a tax filing, a loan application, or telling anyone outside your business a number
  • People decisions — hiring, pay, promotion, discipline, termination
  • Dates and scope you promise — before you commit to a customer

Nothing goes out without a person approving it

Atrovia does not send a message, publish a post, launch a campaign, spend a dollar or write to your books on its own. A person with authority approves it first. That is by design, and it is the most important safeguard in the product.

Where we offer automation or scheduling that reduces how often you are asked, turning it on is your choice — and what goes out under it is still yours.

What Atrovia is not

Atrovia is not an accountant, a bookkeeper, a lawyer, a tax adviser, a financial adviser or an HR professional. Nothing it produces is accounting, tax, legal, investment or employment advice, and using it doesn't create a professional relationship. When a decision has legal, tax or employment consequences, talk to someone qualified who knows your situation.

And your accounting records of record are the ones in your accounting system — not the reports Atrovia builds from them.

Features that involve your people

Atrovia scores performance, recommends when to hire, and shows capacity across your team. These are tools to support your judgement.

You are the employer. You make employment decisions, you are responsible for making them lawfully, and you should tell your team what is being recorded about them. Never make an employment decision on an agent's recommendation alone.

Your data and these agents

  • Your business data is not used to train AI models — not ours, not our providers'.
  • Data is never mixed between businesses. An agent answering a question for you sees only your data.
  • Some responses are produced using third-party AI models under contracts that prohibit training on your data.

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

These features are changing

The agents are experimental and under active development. Their behaviour, wording, accuracy and availability will change as we improve them, and some features are offered as previews. We can't promise any particular result from following a recommendation — no software can, and you should be sceptical of anyone who says otherwise.
