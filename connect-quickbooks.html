Connect your QuickBooks

This is the step that turns your estimates into measured numbers. Before you connect, your margin, your labour cost and your cash position are whatever you told us. After you connect, they come from your books.

Connect to QuickBooks

You'll sign in at Intuit. We never see your QuickBooks password.

What happens when you click

  1. Intuit asks you to sign in and pick which company file to share. You stay on Intuit's site for this — your password never touches Atrovia.
  2. You approve the connection. Intuit shows you exactly what Atrovia is asking for before you agree.
  3. We read your books once to build your first set of numbers, then keep them current on a schedule.
  4. Fin walks you through what changed — usually the moment an owner finds out their real margin isn't the one they've been quoting against.

What we read

  • Profit and loss
  • Balance sheet
  • Chart of accounts
  • Invoices and bills
  • Payroll expense totals
  • Customers and items

What we never do

  • Post transactions to your books
  • Change or delete anything
  • Sell your data
  • Use it for advertising
  • Mix it with another business
  • See your Intuit password

Read-only, and reversible

Atrovia reads your books. It does not write to them. Nothing you see in QuickBooks changes because you connected, and nothing in your accounting is at risk.

You can disconnect at any time — from Settings in Atrovia, or from Apps inside QuickBooks. The moment you do, we stop retrieving data and the access tokens are revoked.

Reconnecting

If your connection lapsed — a password change, an expired authorisation, or a disconnect you want to undo — use the same button. Reconnecting picks up where you left off, and the history we already built stays intact.

If you're connecting a different QuickBooks company than before, tell us first at admin@atrovia.co — one Atrovia business maps to one QuickBooks company, and swapping without a word turns your trend lines into nonsense.

Before you connect

  • You'll need to be an admin on the QuickBooks company. If your bookkeeper holds that, they can complete this step for you.
  • Have the right company selected if you manage more than one in Intuit. This is the single most common mistake, and it reports the wrong business for months before anyone notices.
