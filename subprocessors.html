Sub-processors

Effective 13 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-13

A sub-processor is a company we use to run Atrovia that may handle your data in the process. We keep this list current because you're entitled to know who touches your information, and because your own customers may ask you the same question.

Every provider below is bound by a written agreement requiring them to protect your data, use it only to provide their service to us, and to say nothing about it to anyone else. None of them is permitted to use your business data to train AI models.

Under our agreement, your data is not used to train models, is not retained beyond what's needed to return the response, and is not used for any purpose other than serving your request. This is the commitment in section 5 of the Privacy Policy, and it is contractual rather than a matter of trust.

Payments and communications

These providers are engaged for billing and for the outbound messaging that credits pay for. We publish each one by name as it goes live, and we update this page before a new provider begins handling customer data — never after.

Services you connect — not sub-processors

These are different, and the difference is worth understanding. When you connect QuickBooks or a Google account, you are authorising us to access a service you already hold. They aren't processing data on our behalf; we're reading data on yours.

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — read-only access to the accounting data you authorise. See Privacy Policy §4.
  • Google — Ads, Analytics, Search Console and Business Profile, where you connect them.
  • Meta — advertising accounts, where you connect them.
  • Third-party add-on providers — data and lead services you enable from the App Store. You hold the account, they bill you directly, and their terms govern. See the Add-on & Marketplace Terms.

Each is governed by your own agreement with that company, and you can disconnect any of them at any time in Settings.

Changes to this list

We update this page before a new sub-processor starts handling customer data. If you'd like to be emailed when it changes, tell us at admin@atrovia.co and we'll add you to the notice list.

If you have a data processing addendum with us, the objection process in that DPA applies to any addition. If you'd like a DPA, ask — we'll send one.
